    About MkulimaFresh

    Natural resources, transformed with purpose

    MkulimaFresh is an innovative Tanzanian enterprise turning natural resources into quality products and practical, sustainable solutions.

    Our mission

    We make quality wood products, beehives and furniture through innovative design, skilled craftsmanship and appropriate technology—creating opportunities while making better use of resources.
